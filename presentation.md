## Slide 2: The Problem
**Headline:** The Invisible Threat
**Bullet Points:**
- Urban air pollution is localized; air quality can drastically change from one street to the next.
- Current weather apps only show a single, generalized AQI score for massive metropolitan areas. 
- People blindly walk or commute through highly polluted "micro-zones" daily, resulting in silent, long-term health degradation.
**Visual Idea:** An icon of lungs next to a dark cloud, or a split image showing a clean street next to a highly congested traffic junction.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "When you look at your weather app, it might tell you the air quality in your city is 'Good'. But the reality is that urban pollution is highly localized. A busy intersection or an industrial zone can have hazardous pollution levels, while a park just two streets away is completely clean. The problem is that this danger is invisible, and millions of people commute through these hazardous micro-zones without ever knowing."

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
**Headline:** EcoByte: Localized Environmental Intelligence
**Bullet Points:**
- **Granular Tracking:** We divide cities into high-resolution hexagonal grids to map localized pollution.
- **Health-First Routing:** We don't just calculate how to get you somewhere fastest, we calculate how to get you there safest.
- **Proactive Alerts:** An automated warning system that alerts users to sudden hazardous spikes.
**Visual Idea:** A high-level architectural diagram or three sharp icons (Map grid, Route path, Bell alert).

**Speaker Notes:**
> "EcoByte changes this by making the invisible, visible. We built an intelligent platform that breaks the city down into a high-resolution grid. By processing this localized data, we empower residents to make health-first commuting decisions, while simultaneously providing city admins with real-time hardware diagnostics."

## Slide 4: Feature Spotlight - Command Dashboard
**Headline:** Real-Time Metrics & Lifestyle Intel
**Bullet Points:**
- Live UV Index, Pressure, Humidity, and Temperature reading.
- Dynamic 5-day predictive forecasts for micro-climates.
- **Lifestyle Intel Engine:** Generates on-the-fly recommendations based on current conditions (e.g., 'Indoor Workouts', 'Apply Sunscreen', 'Allergy Risk').
**Visual Idea:** Screenshot of the EcoByte Dashboard highlighting the modular data grid.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "At the core of the platform is the Command Dashboard. It doesn't just show numbers; it translates data into actionable lifestyle intelligence. If UV is high or pollen is spiking, EcoByte automatically advises the user to apply sunscreen or take allergy medication before stepping outside."

---

## Slide 5: Feature Spotlight - AuraPath Routing
**Headline:** AuraPath: The "Cleanest" vs "Fastest" Commute
**Bullet Points:**
- Intelligent navigation engine powered by spatial AQI data.
- Analyzes multiple potential routes to a destination.
- Allows users to physically bypass pollution hotspots, sacrificing just 1 to 2 minutes of travel time for drastically cleaner air.
**Visual Idea:** Screenshot of the AuraPath map, specifically showing the two different paths ("Cleanest" button vs "Fastest" button) and their respective health scores.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "AuraPath is our flagship feature. When navigating, standard GPS apps calculate the fastest route. AuraPath calculates the healthiest route. By taking a 2-minute detour down greener avenues, our engine allows users to avoid heavy toxic exposure from major traffic choke points. It gives the commuter a genuine choice between time and health."

---

## Slide 6: Dual Access - Resident App vs. Admin Portal
**Headline:** Built for Citizens & System Administrators
**Bullet Points:**
- **Resident App:** Clean, user-friendly interface optimized for navigation, alerts, and daily planning.
- **Admin Portal:** Secure backend view displaying active/offline hardware IoT sensors, average system uptimes, and diagnostic capabilities.
**Visual Idea:** A side-by-side comparison of the Login selection screen, showing the Resident (Leaf icon) and the Admin (Server icon).

**Speaker Notes:**
> "The ecosystem is built for two distinct audiences. A resident uses the app seamlessly to navigate and monitor air quality. However, a city administrator logging in has access to the underlying System Status tracker, ensuring that the physical street-level sensors mapping this data remain online and operational."

---

## Slide 7: The Technology Stack
**Headline:** How It's Built
**Bullet Points:**
- **Frontend Framework:** React.js / Vite / Tailwind CSS
- **Design & UI:** Framer Motion for liquid-smooth interactive physics
- **Mapping & Geospatial:** MapLibre GL JS & Turf.js 
- **APIs Integrated:** OpenRouteService (Smart Driving Logic), Open-Meteo (Forecasting), MapTiler (Map Assets), WAQI (Pollution Metrics).
**Visual Idea:** A cluster of the logos of the technologies used.

**Speaker Notes:**
> "To build this, I leveraged Vite and React.js for lightning-fast UI rendering. The interactive mapping is powered by MapLibre alongside Turf.js to geometrically calculate the pollution data within our hexagonal areas. All of this interfaces seamlessly with the OpenRouteService API and localized weather databases, wrapped in a beautiful Tailwind styling."
